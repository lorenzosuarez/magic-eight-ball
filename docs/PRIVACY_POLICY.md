# Privacy Policy for Magic 8 Ball (Wear OS)

Last Updated: April 17, 2026

This privacy policy explains how **Magic 8 Ball** ("the App") handles your data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
The App does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email, or contacts.

## 2. Third-Party Services (Google Gemini AI)
The App uses the Google Gemini API to generate responses. When you interact with the App, the text of your query is sent to Google's servers to generate a response. This data is handled according to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). No personal data is attached to these requests.

## 3. Connectivity
The App requires an active internet connection to communicate with the Gemini API to function.

## 4. Children's Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official Google Play Store contact details provided on the App's store listing page.
