# Delete your Magic AI Ball account

This page is for people who signed in with Google to Magic AI Ball, the Android app by Lorenzo Suarez. If you never signed in, the app has no account for you and there is nothing to delete here. The same is true if you signed in and then chose "Continue without an account" on the profile, or entered a date of birth of someone under 18: the app deleted that account at once, unless it told you that it could not. In that case, try again in the app with a connection, or write to the address below.

## In the app
1. Open Magic AI Ball on your phone or tablet, signed in to the account you want to delete.
2. Open **Options** and choose **Account**.
3. Choose **Delete account** and confirm.

The app may first ask you to sign in with Google once more, to make sure it is you. The account is deleted as soon as you confirm, and this cannot be undone.

## Without the app
Write to the developer at [lorenzosuarez.dev@gmail.com](mailto:lorenzosuarez.dev@gmail.com). Send the message from the email address of the Google Account you signed in with, and say that you want your Magic AI Ball account deleted. The developer answers to that same address to make sure the request is yours, and deletes the account within 30 days of your message. You do not need the app, and you are asked for nothing else.

## What is deleted
- **Your account:** what Google gave the app when you signed in, which is your name, your email address, the address of your profile photo and the account's identifier.
- **Your profile:** your first name, your date of birth and the record of what you agreed to send to the AI service.
- **Your characters:** every character you created, with its name, its description and its look.

The developer keeps no copy of any of it. Google, which keeps this data for the developer, then removes it from its own systems and backups; for what it gave the app when you signed in, this takes up to 180 days.

## What is not deleted
- **What is on your device.** The "Recents" list of your latest questions and answers, including the names of your characters on the answers they gave, and your choices in the app. They are kept only on your device. Delete them with "Delete all" in the list, by clearing the app's data or by uninstalling the app.
- **What was already sent to the AI service.** Google logs requests for a limited time under its own terms, and the developer cannot delete them from there.
- **Crash reports and usage counts.** They are not tied to your account. Google keeps crash reports, and any answer you reported, for 90 days, and usage counts for at most 14 months.
- **Google's record of sign-ins.** The IP addresses used to sign in, which Google keeps for a few weeks.
- **Your Google Account.** Nothing in it changes. You can also remove Magic AI Ball from the apps connected to it, at [myaccount.google.com/connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
- **What you shared.** A picture of an answer that you sent to another app stays wherever you sent it.

## Deleting only part of it
You do not have to delete the account to delete what is in it. In the app you can delete any of your characters where they are listed, change your profile under "Account", and delete entries from "Recents". To have a character deleted or your profile changed without the app, write to the same address and say which.
